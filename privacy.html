Privacy policy · Version 2.0.0 · 16 October 2026

Your camera preview stays on your device.

MeetingLens requests camera access when you choose to start the camera. Camera frames are processed locally in your browser to display the preview, apply your selected adjustments, and create a snapshot when you request one. The app does not upload camera video, automatically record it, or request microphone access.

Camera control

You can stop camera capture using the stop control or Privacy Shield. By default, MeetingLens also stops camera capture when the app is hidden; you can change this preference. Restarting the camera requires your action. Your browser and operating system also provide permission controls.

Settings and presets

MeetingLens stores visual preferences and saved presets in browser-local storage on your device. Preset files you choose to import are read and validated locally. Presets are not synchronized to a cloud account. You can delete saved presets in the app or remove browser site data to clear stored preferences.

Snapshots and exported files

A PNG or JPEG snapshot is downloaded when you request a capture, either immediately or after your selected countdown. A preset file is downloaded when you choose to export it. Downloaded files remain under your control; deleting browser data does not delete files from your Downloads folder.

Sample imagery and AI

The labeled demo portrait is a fictional person created with AI and included as an app asset. The demo does not use your camera. Its six sample backgrounds apply only to that demo portrait. MeetingLens does not send your camera frames to a cloud AI service or perform face identification.

Offline files and hosting

The app caches static files such as the interface, icons, and demo image so the shell can reopen offline after a successful first load. This cache is not a recording of your camera. The app has no account, advertising, or built-in analytics service. The website hosting provider may process ordinary network-request information under its own privacy policy.

Meeting-app scope

This PWA adjusts the preview inside MeetingLens. It does not create a system-wide virtual camera or modify the video sent by other meeting applications.
