Privacy policy · Version 3.2.1 · 17 October 2026

Your camera, photos, and audio stay on your device.

MeetingLens is a local camera studio and photo editor. It has no user account, advertising service, built-in analytics, or cloud inference endpoint. The app does not upload camera frames, microphone audio, imported images, reference photos, or capability reports to its publisher.

Camera and local vision

Camera access is requested when you choose to start it. Frames are processed locally to display the preview, apply adjustments, detect face landmarks for framing and modest refinement, and segment the foreground for room blur or replacement with a selected bundled background. Selecting a scene for a live camera starts loading the bundled local vision models. If the camera is off, model loading waits until you start it. Scene selection does not start the camera or send its frames to a server. Models run in a browser worker, using GPU acceleration for person detection on supported hardware and CPU/WebAssembly fallback. WebGL2 support and renderer availability are checked locally; renderer details are not saved or uploaded. A bounded pair of camera frames is held temporarily in memory so each mask is applied to its matching frame; those buffers are cleared on reset, stop, hiding the app, or Privacy Shield. These tools detect image regions; they do not identify who you are or create a face-recognition account.

You can stop capture using Stop or Privacy Shield. The default camera setting also stops capture when the app is hidden; you can change that camera preference. Camera restart requires your action. Your browser and Windows also provide camera-permission controls. Basic preview bypasses MeetingLens effects, but cannot promise removal of effects already applied by Windows or the camera driver.

Optional microphone check and recording

Microphone permission is separate from camera permission and is requested only when you start the microphone check. The browser can apply supported noise suppression, echo cancellation, and automatic gain control to a local level meter and test recording. No microphone audio is sent to a server or to another meeting app.

Recording begins only when you choose Record and is limited to 60 seconds. A temporary local audio clip can be played, downloaded, or discarded. Stopping the microphone releases its tracks and stops recording; a completed clip remains available until discarded or replaced. Hiding the app or using Privacy Shield releases capture tracks and clears the temporary test clip. Downloaded recordings remain under your control on your device.

Imported photos and whiteboards

Photos and color-reference images you choose are decoded and edited locally. Tone enhancement, color matching, brush inpainting, manual retouching, optional facial-landmark assistance, and whiteboard perspective correction use on-device processing. Up to eight photos are held in the current page, resized to a maximum 2048-pixel edge. Edits are not automatically saved as a cloud album. Exported JPEGs and ZIP sets are downloaded only when you choose to save them.

Settings and presets

Visual preferences and saved presets use browser-local storage. Preset files you import are validated and read locally; they are not synchronized to a cloud account. Delete saved presets in the app or clear the site's browser data to remove stored preferences. Clearing site data may also remove offline files and browser snapshot backups.

Snapshots and optional backup

Snapshots download as PNG or JPEG when you request capture, immediately or after your chosen countdown. Automatic backup is off until you opt in for the current session. You can select a writable local folder in a supported browser, or enable browser-local IndexedDB backups. Browser backups keep the latest 50 stills, with controls to download or delete them. Folder backups write to the location you explicitly permit and remain there until you remove them. Turning backup off does not delete previously saved images.

Browser storage can be cleared, evicted, or become unavailable; it is not guaranteed archival storage. Download files and important backups to a location you control. Deleting browser site data does not delete files from Downloads or a selected folder.

QR content and clipboard

QR decoding runs locally on the current preview using a browser detector or the bundled jsQR decoder. Decoded content is displayed for review. The app writes it to the system clipboard only when you choose Copy, or enable the option to copy newly decoded links automatically. The app does not open decoded links automatically. Clipboard access is subject to browser and operating-system permissions; clipboard contents may be available to other software under their own permissions.

Windows settings and capability reports

The Windows camera page opens camera or sound settings only when you click a settings link. A capability report you choose to import is validated and shown locally in that tab, without upload or persistent storage by the report viewer. The separate native companion's report contains device/capability information, a hashed device identifier, and available modes; it contains no captured video or audio. A report is a snapshot, not a live connection or automatic control of Windows.

Sample imagery

The labeled sample person is fictional and AI-generated. The six bundled interior photographs can be used behind the sample or as locally composited backgrounds for the live camera preview. Using the sample or choosing a scene does not activate the camera. Live replacement and room blur use local segmentation; the app retains the actual room while analysis is loading, unavailable or too old to use.

Offline files and hosting

The app caches its static interface, icons, sample imagery, vision models, and QR decoder after a successful first load. These cached files are app resources, not recordings of you. The website hosting provider receives ordinary page and static-file requests and may process normal request metadata under its own privacy policy. Local camera, photo, microphone, and report contents are not included in those requests by MeetingLens.

Meeting-app scope

The standalone PWA processes its own preview and local test audio. The separate Windows MeetingLens Camera application embeds the bundled studio and, after you start meeting output, transfers processed video through local shared memory and a local named pipe to its virtual camera. Meeting applications receive that video when you select the MeetingLens camera in their camera settings. MeetingLens does not upload those frame buffers, save them as recordings, or provide a virtual microphone. Privacy Shield and stopped input clear output; a stalled connection expires its last frame. Meeting applications handle the video they receive under their own privacy policies. The Windows component has separate installation requirements and is not installed by the PWABuilder package.
