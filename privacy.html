Privacy policy · Version 1.0

MeetingLens keeps camera processing on your device.

MeetingLens requests camera access only after you choose Start camera preview. Camera frames are processed in the browser on your device to display the preview and selected visual adjustments.

What MeetingLens stores

  • Your selected visual settings are saved in your browser's local storage so they remain available next time.
  • MeetingLens does not upload, stream, sell, or share camera video.
  • A snapshot is downloaded only when you explicitly select Save snapshot.

What MeetingLens does not do

  • It does not record the camera automatically.
  • It does not create a system-wide virtual camera in this PWA version.
  • It does not use cloud AI services or require an account.
