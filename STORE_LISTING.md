## Full description

### English

MeetingLens helps you prepare your webcam before a call, class, or presentation.

Open a live camera preview, refine color and lighting, choose a natural look, check your framing, and compare the adjusted image with the original—all in one simple Windows app.

MeetingLens is designed for people who want a more polished camera presence without changing their natural appearance. Use it to review your setup before an online meeting, teaching session, or presentation.

Use MeetingLens to:

- Preview your camera before a meeting
- Adjust natural color, contrast, and lighting
- Choose subtle visual looks
- Check face position and headroom
- Try background and scene preview tools
- Use Privacy Shield to stop and obscure the visible preview instantly
- Compare the original view with your adjusted preview
- Save an enhanced snapshot only when you choose

**Privacy and compatibility:** MeetingLens requests camera access only with your permission. A working webcam is required. This PWA improves the preview inside MeetingLens; it does not create a system-wide virtual camera or modify video in other meeting apps.
