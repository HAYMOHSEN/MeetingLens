MeetingLens 3.2.3 — Head edge detail update

For your existing MeetingLens 3.2.2 repository. Keep all existing models, images, icons and other files. This is a small update, not a full app or Windows installer.

2. Commit to main and wait for GitHub Pages deployment to succeed in Actions.
