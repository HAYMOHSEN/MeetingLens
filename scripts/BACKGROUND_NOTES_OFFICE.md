# Professional office background assets

These images depict AI-generated fictional rooms. Created with the built-in image generation tool for MeetingLens sample-preview portrait compositing. They contain no people and are not photographs of actual offices.

Both images were visually inspected, then mechanically resized and center-cropped to 1600 × 900. The PNG files preserve full-resolution integration sources; WebP files are opaque RGB, encoded at quality 85 for fast local loading. No content was painted, retouched, or otherwise edited after generation.

## Executive office

- Files: `assets/demo/backgrounds/executive-office.png`, `assets/demo/backgrounds/executive-office.webp`
- Prompt: Premium modern executive office background photographed with natural realism. Warm walnut architectural details at the side edges, cream plaster walls, subtle recessed lighting, one tidy bookshelf at the far side edge, floor-to-ceiling daylight window at one side, restrained muted taupe, gray and wood palette. Fictional premium office. Wide 16:9 landscape image from the eye-level laptop webcam viewpoint of a seated speaker facing camera; show the background behind the speaker. Central sixty percent is an uncluttered neutral cream wall for compositing a head-and-shoulders portrait. Details and furniture at outer edges, credible perspective, high-end architectural photography, detailed natural materials, quiet professional setting, soft neutral daylight. Empty room; no people, faces, foreground chair, prominent table, text, logos, watermarks, screens or signage. Very subtle realistic depth of field. Opaque image.
