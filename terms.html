MeetingLens terms of use

MeetingLens is a local camera-preview and preparation tool. Use it responsibly and follow the rules that apply to your camera use, organization, and downloaded images.

Permissions and local files

You choose whether to grant camera access and when to capture or export files. You can stop the camera in the app or revoke permission in your browser. Keep backups of presets and snapshots that matter to you; browser-local data can be removed by your browser settings or storage cleanup.

Feature scope

The PWA does not create a virtual camera device or modify video in third-party meeting applications. The six background choices apply only to the fictional AI demo portrait, which is labeled in the interface. Framing guides are manual composition aids, not face detection or a measured readiness assessment.

Compatibility

Live preview requires a compatible camera, browser support, and your permission. Actual resolution and performance depend on your hardware and browser. A requested quality setting is a preference, not a guarantee that a camera can provide that resolution.
