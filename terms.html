MeetingLens terms of use

MeetingLens provides a local camera preview, photo editing, whiteboard correction, and optional microphone-check tools. Use the app and exported files responsibly, with permission to capture or edit the material involved.

Permissions and files

You choose camera and microphone access separately, when to record a local test, when to capture/export a file, and whether to enable snapshot backups or QR-link copying. You can stop capture in the app and revoke permissions in your browser or Windows. Privacy Shield stops camera and microphone capture. Keep copies of important files; browser-local storage and temporary page data can be removed or lost.

Local effects and image quality

Face tracking, person segmentation, and face-landmark assistance depend on the image and can fail or produce visible artifacts. Photo tone enhancement, color matching, brush inpainting, and whiteboard corner suggestions are aids that require review. Inpainting is not generative scene reconstruction, and the app cannot guarantee recovery of clipped highlights, exact reference colors, perfect skin/teeth changes, or correct automatic board corners. Undo and manual corrections are provided where applicable.

The sample person is fictional, AI-generated, and labeled. The six interior scenes can replace the room in the live MeetingLens preview using local person segmentation, or appear behind the sample. Live replacement and room blur require successfully loaded models and usable current analysis; the actual room remains visible while analysis is loading, unavailable or stale. Use real background disables replacement and blur. Original, Basic preview and zero enhancement intensity bypass this app's processing; they do not force raw sensor output or remove Windows/driver effects.

Meeting apps and Windows features

The PWA does not register a system virtual camera or microphone and does not route processed media into third-party meeting apps. Browser microphone processing is distinct from Windows AI Voice Focus. Eye-contact correction and Windows Studio Effects require compatible Windows hardware and are handled by the separate native utility or Windows settings. The browser models use CPU/WebAssembly with a required WebGL2 texture bridge, not an app-selected NPU.

Imported native capability reports are read-only snapshots and can become stale. Separate native source projects and virtual-camera reference sources require their own build, hardware validation, packaging, and integration. They are not part of the PWABuilder PWA package.

Compatibility

Available camera modes, zoom, frame rates, audio processing, clipboard access, folder backup, offline caching, and performance depend on browser, operating system, hardware, drivers, permissions, and storage. Requested quality preferences do not guarantee a camera can deliver them. Multi-app camera support is a Windows/device capability, not a universal promise from MeetingLens.
